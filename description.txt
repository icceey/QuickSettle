QuickSettle instantly settles all flowing liquids in your world, letting them rush to their final positions at once — eliminating lag from large-scale liquid movement and speeding up your terraforming projects.

Usage:
  Send the exact message "1" in in-game chat to trigger it, or run "/settle". (The send-"1" shortcut is enabled by default. If you're concerned about accidentally triggering it, you can disable the send-"1" shortcut in the mod config and still use the command.)
  

---

QuickSettle 可以立即结算世界中所有流动的液体，让所有流动中的液体快速流动到最终位置，消除大规模液体流动带来的卡顿，并加速你的地形改造工程。

使用方法：
  在游戏内聊天框中发送 "1" 即可触发，或者输入 "/settle" 命令。(如果你担心正常聊天时发送"1"会误触，可以在模组配置页面关闭发送"1"的快捷触发，关闭后你仍然可以通过命令触发。)
